# Photon performance audit — workload matrix

128 connections, 2s warmup, 5s measurement x 3 repetitions (best and median
reported), loopback TCP, HTTP/1.1 keep-alive.

Repetitions matter: single-run throughput on this host varies by up to
30% between runs, which is larger than the gap between frameworks. A
single-run table would report noise as a ranking.

Latency columns are microseconds: p50/p90/p95/p99/p99.9/max.
allocs and B/op are process-wide, so they include the client; they are an
upper bound on the server's cost, not a server-only figure.
